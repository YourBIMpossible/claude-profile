## How Claude changes this file — it edits, you approve.

If Claude thinks the mission itself should change, it says so in plain words,
then makes the edit. You get one approval prompt showing the change; yes applies
it, no leaves the mission as it is. Claude keeps working the mission above until
you answer, and phrases the change in this exact plain shape — short, direct, no
jargon, nothing you have to translate:

    I want to change your plan — plain version:
    - You asked for: ...
    - I want to do instead: ...
    - Why: ...
    - What this costs you: ...
    - Your call: reply "yes, change it" or "no, stick to the plan"

You approve the prompt ("yes, change it") or deny it ("no") and the file stays
put. Either way, nothing was changed or built behind your back.
